Admin-led onboarding

Null Corp configures the business, rehearses the edge cases, and holds activation until the system passes its launch checks. Your team sees every decision before Ava takes a live call.

Start the setup conversation ↗
  1. 01
    Invite and workspace

    We create your Frontdesk account.

    Your owner receives a single-use invitation. The workspace starts in draft, with no live routing and no accidental calls.

  2. 02
    Business profile

    Teach Ava the business.

    We load services, hours, service area, policies, FAQs, pricing boundaries, holiday behavior, and emergency rules.

  3. 03
    Phone and receptionist

    Choose how calls arrive and how Ava speaks.

    Use a dedicated number, forward your existing line, or route only after-hours calls. Then set Ava's name, voice, greeting, questions, silence behavior, and transfer rules.

  4. 04
    Booking and notifications

    Define what happens after the call.

    Connect Google Calendar for direct booking, use staff-confirmed requests, or capture leads only. Choose which events reach email, Slack, SMS, or a signed webhook.

  5. 05
    Rehearsal

    Try to break it before a customer can.

    Run scripted cases for greeting, silence, FAQs, rambling callers, lead capture, booking failure, after-hours behavior, transfer, and tool outages. Then talk to Ava in a live browser rehearsal.

  6. 06
    Approval and activation

    Launch only when the evidence is current.

    Frontdesk checks business data, phone routing, receptionist settings, booking safety, integration health, notifications, and configuration stability. Any critical edit makes the old result stale and requires a new check.

Questions worth asking

Before Ava answers.

Does Ava pretend to be a person?+

No. If a caller asks, Ava says she is the business's virtual receptionist, then gets back to helping.

What happens when Ava does not know an answer?+

She says she does not want to give incorrect information, captures the question, and routes it for human follow-up. She does not invent prices, policies, availability, names, or customer records.

Can we keep our current number?+

Yes. Frontdesk can work with a dedicated number, forwarding from an existing business line, later porting, or an after-hours forwarding pattern. The final route depends on your carrier and call flow.

Can Ava book appointments herself?+

Yes, when direct booking is enabled and a writable Google Calendar passes connection checks. You can also start in staff-confirmed request mode or lead-only mode.

What does my team manage after launch?+

Your managers can review calls and transcripts, work leads, schedule follow-ups, manage appointments, tune business and receptionist settings, inspect usage and billing, and read account and system notices.
